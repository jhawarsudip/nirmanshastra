Interior Quality Tips — Know What to Demand From Your Contractor (Free to Read)

🔲 Flooring Quality Checks

  • Always check tile shade lot number — same shade code only
  • Lay tiles on dry surface with proper slope in wet areas (1:100)
  • Use tile spacers for consistent joints — never uneven gaps
  • Test vitrified tiles: water drop should bead off (low absorption)
  • Skirting tiles should be same grade as floor — demand matching

🏗️ Modular Kitchen Red Flags

  • Always ask for BWP (Boiling Water Proof) plywood, not MR grade
  • Check hinge brand — Hettich/Hafele vs local hinges last 10× longer
  • Countertop must have 6mm backing board under tiles/granite
  • Ask for proper back panel — thin HDF is not acceptable
  • Demand anti-slam soft-close on all shutters and drawers

🔧 Contractor Warning Signs

  • Refusing to break down material and labour separately = red flag
  • No written contract with material brand specifications = red flag
  • Changing brands mid-project without prior approval — reject
  • Demanding >30% advance payment before work starts — negotiate down
  • No site protection (dust sheets) during other trades = unprofessional

💎 Where Splurging Pays Off

  • Main door: You see it every day. Worth spending premium here.
  • Kitchen cabinet hardware: Cheap hinges fail in 3–5 years.
  • Master bathroom: Premium tiles and fittings have 20+ year life.
  • Living room flooring: Marble here with basic elsewhere = smart spend.
  • False ceiling lighting: Cove lighting transforms a room completely.

💡 Where to Save Without Regret

  • Bedroom flooring: vitrified is fine — nobody sees it under furniture
  • Bathroom geyser position: standard over premium — same function
  • Servant/utility room: basic ceramic is completely adequate
  • Interior door finish: flush doors with good veneer look premium
  • Balcony ceiling: skip false ceiling here — not worth the cost

⛔ Never Cut Corners On

  • Waterproofing: bathroom and terrace — leaks ruin structural slabs
  • Electrical wire behind walls: must be IS 732 compliant (was in ElectroPro)
  • Plumbing slopes: wrong slope = permanent blockage (was in PlumbPro)
  • Tile adhesive: never lay tile on dry cement — tiles pop in 2–3 years
  • Primer before paint: skipping primer = paint peels in 2 monsoons

Quantities are estimated from standard room proportions and average material usage factors. Rates are India 2026 averages and vary significantly by city, brand choice, and market conditions. This estimate does not include GST (18% on most interior services and materials), furniture, appliances, curtains, or any item not listed.
