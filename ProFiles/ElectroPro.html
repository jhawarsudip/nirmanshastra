Electrical Quality & Safety Reminders — IS Code Based (Free to Read)

🌍 Earthing (IS 3043:2018)

  • Minimum 2 earth pits for any building — mandatory
  • Earth wire must be continuous — no joints underground
  • Test earth resistance annually — target <1 Ohm
  • Use GI or copper earth wire only — no aluminium
  • Maintain moisture in earth pits — water monthly

🛡️ RCCB — Mandatory (IS 12640)

  • 30mA RCCB mandatory for bathroom and kitchen circuits
  • 30mA RCCB mandatory for all outdoor circuits
  • Test RCCB with test button every 3 months
  • RCCB must trip within 40ms at rated current
  • Never bypass RCCB — it saves lives in shock events

🔌 MCB Sizing (IS 8828:2007)

  • Never replace a tripped MCB with a higher rating
  • MCB breaking capacity: min 6kA for residential
  • Never use fuse wire in place of MCB — illegal
  • All DBs must have a main incomer MCB or RCCB
  • Label every MCB in DB — circuit name and area

⚡ Wire Colour Coding (IS 732:2019)

  • Phase wire: Red (L1), Yellow (L2), Blue (L3)
  • Neutral wire: Black — always, no exceptions
  • Earth wire: Green/Green-Yellow — never carry current
  • Never reverse neutral and earth at socket outlet
  • All wires must follow IS 732 colour code

🧪 Testing Before Energising (IE Rules)

  • Insulation resistance (megger): min 1 MΩ per circuit
  • Earth loop impedance test on all circuits
  • Polarity check — L and N not reversed at outlets
  • RCD operating time test at 5× rated current
  • All tests mandatory before electricity board connection

🚿 Wet Area Safety (IS 732)

  • No socket outlets inside bathrooms — IS 732 Cl.10.4
  • Bathroom light fitting: IP44 minimum (splash-proof)
  • Geyser: double pole isolator switch outside bathroom
  • No metal switch plates in bathrooms — plastic only
  • All bathroom/kitchen circuits: 30mA RCCB mandatory
