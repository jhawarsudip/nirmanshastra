Plumbing Quality & Safety Reminders — IS Code Based (Free to Read)

💧 Pipe Slopes — IS 1742:1983

  • Waste pipes (basin, bath): min slope 1:40 (2.5%)
  • Soil pipe (WC): min slope 1:80 (1.25%) up to 1:40
  • Underground drain: min 1:80, recommended 1:40
  • Wrong slope = permanent blockage — never compromise
  • All horizontal drain pipes must be self-cleansing velocity

🌊 Wet Wall Clustering — NBC 2016 Part 9

  • Stack all bathroom wet walls vertically on same riser
  • Never route soil pipe horizontally more than 1.8m without drop
  • Kitchen and bathroom on same wall reduces pipe run by 40%
  • Avoid crossing soil pipe under slab — nightmare to repair
  • Leave inspection chamber at every direction change

🔥 Hot Water Pipes — IS 15328:2003

  • CPVC is mandatory for hot water — UPVC fails above 60°C
  • GI pipes corrode in 8–12 years — CPVC lasts 25+ years
  • Hot water pipe: insulate to reduce heat loss
  • Keep geyser outlet pipe short — max 1.5m to first tap
  • Use pressure relief valve (PRV) on every geyser — mandatory

🌿 Water Conservation — NBC 2016

  • Use dual-flush WC cistern (3L + 6L) — saves 40% water
  • Aerator on taps reduces flow from 12 LPM to 6 LPM
  • Sensor taps in common areas reduce wastage by 70%
  • Rainwater harvesting: required in many states by law
  • Check local municipal authority for mandatory RWH rules

⛔ Common Plumbing Disasters to Avoid

  • Never bury GI pipe in concrete — corrodes, leaks in 5 years
  • Never route soil pipe inside bedroom wall — noise + odour
  • Anti-siphon traps mandatory on all basins — IS 1742
  • No direct connection between soil pipe and rain pipe
  • Always pressure-test supply pipes before closing walls

🔧 Pipe Pressure Test — IS 1172

  • Test at 1.5× working pressure for 30 min minimum
  • CPVC: working pressure 150 PSI, test at 225 PSI
  • Test before plastering — never after wall is closed
  • Mark all buried pipe routes on an as-built drawing
  • Get plumber to sign off on pressure test results

🛢️ Tank Sizing (IS 1172:1993)

  • Overhead tank: minimum 1 day storage for residential
  • Sump tank: 2–3× overhead tank volume (for pump cycles)
  • Tanks must be covered — mosquito breeding risk
  • Clean overhead tank every 6 months — IS 10500
  • Tank inlet must have float valve — prevents overflow

🏗️ Septic Tank — IS 2470:1985

  • Minimum distance from building: 3m (IS 2470)
  • Minimum from water source (well/bore): 15m
  • Soak pit area: 10 sqm per 1000 LPCD for medium soil
  • Desludge every 2–3 years — do not neglect
  • Septic tank outlet must have inspection chamber
