🏠 Internal wall plaster
Cement Plaster (standard)
12mm, 1:4 cement:sand. The IS 1661:1972 standard. Most common across India.
IS 1661:1972 · 0.153 bags cement/sqm
Gypsum Plaster
No water curing needed. Faster. Smooth finish. Premium flats and commercial spaces. 12.5mm thick.
IS 2547:1976 · Growing in metros
Waterproof Cement Plaster
Standard cement + waterproofing admixture (Dr Fixit/Cico). Used in wet areas and basement walls.
IS 1661 + IS 2645 · Wet areas recommended
No Internal Plaster
For AAC blocks (skim coat only), gypsum drywall, or exposed brick design. Saves significant cost.
AAC walls need only 3mm skim coat

Masonry Quality Reminders — IS Code Based (Free to Read)

🧱 Brick Quality (IS 1077 + IS 3102)

  • Soak clay bricks 8–12 hours before laying — dry bricks destroy mortar
  • Min compressive strength: Class 3.5 for non-load-bearing, Class 7.5 for seismic zones IV-V
  • Never use under-burnt (yellowish) bricks — they dissolve in moisture
  • Test: ring test — good brick gives metallic ring, bad brick thuds
  • English bond for 9" walls, Stretcher bond for 4.5" partitions

🏭 Fly Ash Brick (IS 12894:2002)

  • Check BIS certification — uncertified fly ash bricks are common in market
  • Compressive strength min 10 N/mm² for Grade 2
  • Water absorption max 12% — test before use
  • No need to soak like clay brick — less water absorption means less pre-wetting
  • Requires ~30% less cement in mortar — adjust your consumption estimates

⬛ Hollow/Solid Block (IS 2185:2005)

  • Grade A: min 4 N/mm² (use for external and structural walls)
  • Grade B: min 2 N/mm² (use only for non-structural partitions)
  • Always fill block cavities with mortar at lintel and sill levels
  • Use M4 mortar (1:6) minimum — M6 (1:4) near openings
  • Do not cut blocks excessively — plan wall layout to minimize cuts

🏗️ AAC Block Rules (IS 2185 Pt.3)

  • AAC = infill material in RCC frame ONLY — not load-bearing in Zone IV-V
  • Use only manufacturer-specified thin joint adhesive — NOT cement mortar
  • Protect from rain during construction — AAC is highly absorbent
  • External AAC walls: use polymer-based plaster (not cement) to prevent cracking
  • Check manufacturer density: 550–650 kg/m³ is standard

⛔ Waterproofing Absolutes (IS 2645:2003)

  • Terrace WP: mandatory before any flooring — test for 48 hours with ponded water
  • Bath sunken: WP before backfill — most failures happen due to skipping this
  • Never use local/unknown brand compounds — use Dr Fixit, Fosroc, BASF, or Pidilite
  • Apply 2 coats of any WP compound — single coat = guaranteed failure
  • BBC: minimum 75mm thick, minimum 1:8 slope to outlet

🏠 Plaster Quality (IS 1661:1972)

  • Internal: 12mm two coat minimum (6+6) — never single coat on rough block
  • External: 15mm two coat — richer mix (1:4) for first coat
  • Chicken wire mesh at RCC-to-brick junction mandatory — prevents crack line
  • Cure plaster minimum 7 days with wet gunny or polythene
  • Gypsum plaster: no curing but protect from moisture for 24 hours

🪜 Seismic Provisions (IS 4326:1993)

  • Zone IV-V: RCC bands at plinth, lintel and sill levels — IS 4326 Cl.8.4
  • Maximum wall length between cross-walls: 6m — IS 4326
  • Openings: max 50% of wall length — IS 4326
  • Reinforced brickwork recommended for load-bearing in Zone IV-V
  • AAC block: use only as infill — not load-bearing in high seismic zones

💧 Mortar Mix (IS 2212:1991)

  • Non-load-bearing: M2 mortar (1:6 cement:sand) minimum
  • Load-bearing Zone III+: M4 (1:4) or M6 (1:3) — IS 2212 Table 1
  • Never use sea sand — chlorides destroy brickwork over time
  • W/C ratio: 0.5–0.6 — wet enough to spread, not watery
  • Never add extra water to stiffened mortar — discard and mix fresh
